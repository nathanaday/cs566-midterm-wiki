---
name: one-plan
description: Change the wiki only through one change plan.
---
Every change to `wiki/topics/` and `wiki/sources/` goes through `wiki_change`, as one plan of creates, modifies, and removes. The tool proposes the plan as a change document; a person approves it in Obsidian, and only then does the wiki change. Your work ends when the plan is proposed: do not wait for the approval, and never ask for it. Never write those notes yourself, never change the original that a source embeds or links, never change a `## Notes` section, and never edit the session document.
