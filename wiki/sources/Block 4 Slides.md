## Summary

The CSCI 566 deck "Block 4: Convolutional Neural Networks", by the instructor Yue Zhao. It has 312 PDF pages. It opens with Ph.D. application advice and a recap of neural networks, then traces CNNs from the visual cortex to LeNet and AlexNet. It builds the four CNN layer types (convolutional, pooling, fully connected, softmax), recaps activation functions, and covers batch normalization. It then walks through the ILSVRC winners (VGGNet, ResNet), efficient designs (MobileNets, EfficientNet), and 2026 practice with pretrained encoders. The last third covers ViT patches, CLIP and contrastive training, visual instruction tuning, SigLIP 2, agentic vision, detection, and application demos, and ends with the move to RNNs and GNNs. Most slides credit Stanford CS 231n or Marc'Aurelio Ranzato. Locators are PDF page numbers.
