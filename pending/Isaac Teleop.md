## What it is

An NVIDIA framework for robotic learning. An operator controls the robot remotely, which collects real-world data (Guest Lecture 9-21 Notes (part 1), § New Entities).

## Facts

- Purpose: remote operation of the robot to collect real-world data (Guest Lecture 9-21 Notes (part 1), § New Entities).
- The same lecture said, under VLA, that lighting conditions have a significant effect on policy training, and that some work goes into modeling lighting accurately in simulation (Guest Lecture 9-21 Notes (part 1), § New Entities).
- Synthesis: teleoperation gives the robot trajectories that [[Vision Language Action Models]] can train on.
